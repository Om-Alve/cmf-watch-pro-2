Shared rules. Black AMOLED background, the stock CMF grey (#282C30) for surfaces, one accent (Faceclaw lobster orange) used only for the command just sent and for problems. Every design reuses the eight Bluetooth keys the remote already sends, so the phone side does not change. The watch cannot see the glasses, so no design shows the glasses' current app; it shows what the watch knows: time, watch battery, the phone link, and the last command. A short vibration on each command is assumed for all of them.
